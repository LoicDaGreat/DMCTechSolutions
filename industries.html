Mining

Engineered components and fabrication solutions built for the demanding conditions of Africa's mining sector.

  • Structural steel fabrication for plant and infrastructure.
  • Reverse engineering and prototype development for critical mining assets.

We support mining operations with precision-manufactured parts, project engineering and industrial consulting — improving productivity, reliability and safety on site.

Bulk Materials Handling

Specialist engineering for conveyor systems, transfer chutes and bulk handling infrastructure across industrial plants and mining operations.

From design through to fabrication and installation support, we deliver reliable materials handling solutions.

  • Conveyor systems and component manufacturing.
  • Transfer chute design and optimisation.

Manufacturing & Industrial Plants

Precision laser cutting, CNC bending and sheet metal fabrication for OEM manufacturers and industrial plant operators.

  • Prototype development and production-ready manufacturing drawings.

We help manufacturing clients improve operational performance through precision-engineered components and reliable production support.
